# Relational Link Unification (RLU): Master Proof Status

**Audit date:** 24 July 2026  
**Core version under evaluation:** **RLU-C4 / Cartan-CDT**

## Executive verdict

RLU is **not yet proved as a fundamental theory of nature**. It has, however, advanced beyond a loose conceptual proposal.

The strongest defensible statement is:

> **RLU-C4 is a mathematically defined finite Cartan regulator with exact local gauge covariance, a controlled classical GR/TEGR limit on fixed four-dimensional complexes, and the correct two-helicity free quantum limit. Its interacting, background-free quantum continuum limit has not been established.**

The entire proof program now turns on one load-bearing question:

> **Does a fully coupled Cartan-CDT measure possess a continuous, regulator-independent, single-metric critical trajectory whose Lorentzian spectrum contains exactly one massless spin-2 particle with two positive-norm helicities and no light scalar, vector, torsion, relative-metric, or higher-derivative ghost modes?**

Until that question is answered positively, the matter, beyond-GR, throat, atemporal, and transport sectors remain **conditional effective constructions**, not predictions derived from the microscopic theory.

The original polar-link idea,

\[
Z_{ij}=H_{ij}U_{ij},
\]

correctly identifies a unitary gauge transporter, but a positive-Hermitian factor does not by itself determine a Lorentzian soldering form. The surviving gravitational core is therefore Cartan-geometric.

On a causal oriented four-complex or four-dimensional CDT triangulation:

\[
V_i\in S^4,
\qquad
G_{ij}\in SO(5)

in the Euclidean regulator, with Lorentzian continuation to an appropriate noncompact Cartan group. Define

\[
P_i=I-V_iV_i^T,
\]

\[
e_{ij}=P_i(G_{ij}V_j-V_i).
\]

The projector has rank four, so the local tangent dimension is selected by

\[
SO(5)\rightarrow SO(4).
\]

This means **RLU-C4 selects four dimensions microscopically; it does not yet derive the number four dynamically**.

### Intrinsic calculus

Transported coframe increments define intrinsic displacements. A mixed-moment matrix inversion gives a local covariant derivative, and translational/rotational loop holonomies yield torsion and curvature. The continuum gravity action is represented by a MacDowell-Mansouri/Einstein-Cartan branch or, in a teleparallel rewriting, the TEGR torsion invariant.

### Required single-metric continuum

A CDT triangulation already carries Regge metric data. Independent Cartan coframes otherwise create a second metric. The only strict two-helicity continuum therefore requires either:

\[
m_{\rm rel}\,\xi\rightarrow\infty,
\]

or

\[
Z_C/Z_R\rightarrow0,
\]

or exact kinematic identification of the two metric structures.

For finite complexes with compact Euclidean variables, the bosonic configuration domain is compact. A real continuous action therefore defines a finite positive bosonic Boltzmann measure. This proves regulator existence, not an interacting continuum or full chiral positivity.

## 6. The current core action must be frozen before further claims

A strict two-helicity production candidate is

\[
S_{\rm core}
=
S_{\rm CDT}
+S_{\rm match}
+S_T
+S_{\rm MM}
+S_{\rm reg}.
\]

Here:

- \(S_{\rm match}\) locks Cartan Gram data to CDT simplex geometry or drives the relative metric infinitely heavy;
- \(S_T\) suppresses physical torsion at continuum scales;
- \(S_{\rm MM}\) supplies the Einstein-Cartan structure;
- \(S_{\rm reg}\) stabilizes the cutoff theory but must leave no physical curvature-squared pole.

The former Wilson term can be used only if its renormalized physical coefficient vanishes in continuum units. A cleaner alternative is a regulator whose surviving curvature-squared contribution is topological or provably irrelevant.

### Two architectural choices

#### Conservative embedding

Impose exact metric compatibility. The Cartan sector becomes an orientation/connection completion of CDT and cannot create a second metric. This is the fastest route to a consistent two-helicity embedding, but it may flow to ordinary CDT with an irrelevant spectator sector.

#### Fundamental Cartan trajectory

Allow an independent Cartan metric microscopically and prove that its relative mode decouples dynamically at criticality. This is more distinctively RLU, but it is substantially harder and currently unsupported by data.

A found two-helicity fixed point would prove a viable quantum-gravity universality class. It would not, by itself, prove that RLU is distinct from or superior to CDT/GR.

## 7. Where the other RLU branches stand

### Beyond-GR branch

A scalar-Gauss-Bonnet plus cubic-Weyl EFT contains GR exactly and supports calculable black-hole effects. The exact scalar profile and conditional mass-independent cross-channel relation are valid properties of that EFT. They become RLU predictions only if the microscopic critical theory calculates the coefficients and their signs.

Current status: **consistent conditional EFT, not derived RLU phenomenology**.

### Matter branch

Overlap/domain-wall methods and defect binding show a plausible route. Missing are a dynamical vacuum manifold, mirror decoupling, a non-Abelian chiral measure on the dynamical irregular complex, three generations, Yukawa structure, and masses/mixing.

Current status: **mechanism demonstrated; unification not derived**.

### Throat/adjacency branch

Finite defect-channel Hamiltonians transfer quantum amplitudes and pass selected quadratic tests. The full arbitrary-background gravitational constraint, microscopic derivation, macroscopic stress-energy transport, and continuum origin are missing. Generic strict two-helicity Cartan-CDT also requires relative spin-2 decoupling, which places a throat with a finite massive spin-2 sector outside the core theory unless treated as a separate low-energy excitation above a proven cutoff.

Current status: **toy/effective construction; not a consequence of core RLU**.

### Atemporal and anomalous-motion branch

Relational clock constructions are mathematically viable. Literal time circles and unrestricted FTL are not. None of this supplies evidence that anomalous aerial reports are relational transport.

Current status: **conceptual side branch, not load-bearing evidence**.

- only first-order or Gaussian transitions;
- no diverging correlation length;
- branched-polymer or crumpled geometry;

- failure of reflection positivity/Lorentzian reconstruction;

## 10. Final standing

### What has been proved

RLU-C4 can be formulated as a finite, intrinsic, locally gauge-covariant Cartan regulator. On fixed four-dimensional structures it reproduces the classical GR/TEGR kinetic content and has a correct two-helicity free quantum limit.

### What has not been proved

No interacting background-free continuum theory has been constructed; no universal two-helicity Cartan-CDT trajectory has been observed; no complete chiral Standard Model has been derived; no RLU-specific beyond-GR coefficient has been computed; and no experiment has selected RLU over GR or competing quantum-gravity approaches.

### The honest one-sentence status

> **RLU is presently a serious but unproved candidate regulator and research program—not yet a completed unified theory, not yet a demonstrated successor to general relativity, and not yet an empirically supported description of nature.**
